## CAPSTONE 17 — Hallucination Benchmark: Testing Free Chatbots

## Project Overview
This project evaluates the reliability of free AI chatbots when answering technical questions related to Computer Science and Engineering (CSE).

## Project Question
How reliable are free AI chatbots on technical facts, and how can users detect hallucinations?

## Objectives
- Study AI hallucinations, their causes, and types.
- Prepare 40 verifiable CSE questions.
- Compare answers from three AI chatbots.
- Verify chatbot answers using reliable primary sources.
- Calculate accuracy scores and classify errors.
- Create charts, reports, and a presentation.
- Develop a fact-checking checklist for AI users.

## Target Audience
CSE students and faculty who use AI tools for learning and technical research.

## Tools
- ChatGPT
- Google Gemini
- A third free AI chatbot
- Google Sheets
- Python
- Reliable official documentation and research papers

## Project Workflow
1. Research AI hallucinations.
2. Prepare the CSE question bank.
3. Collect answers from three chatbots.
4. Verify answers against reliable sources.
5. Record results and calculate accuracy.
6. Analyze errors and create charts.
7. Prepare the final report and presentation.

## Expected Deliverables
- Research report
- Fact-checking checklist
- 40 CSE questions and answer key
- Chatbot comparison and scored results
- Charts and findings article
- Six-slide presentation
- Python script or spreadsheet
- Prompt library
- Verification report
- Before-and-after analysis
- Final demonstration

## Responsible AI
AI-generated answers must be independently verified. Results will be dated because chatbot capabilities can change over time.
